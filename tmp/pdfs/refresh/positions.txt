Planetary Positions
Your planetary positions record where the Sun, Moon and planets were at the moment of your birth.
In astrology, each represents a different part of your inner world, from identity and emotions to
communication, relationships and motivation.
The zodiac sign describes how those themes are expressed, while the house shows the area of life in
which they unfold. Together, these placements form the foundation of your chart and the
interpretations throughout this report.
