                      THE TWELVE HOUSES
     The twelve houses of one's chart represent different life areas where planetary energies manifest.
     Each house governs specific themes and experiences in one's life journey.




 First House - Self and Identity
 House of Self


     HOUSE I                                                 ♉ TAURUS 16.2°
     Identity, appearance, self-expression, beginnings,
     and the way you meet life.                            Fixed · Earth

     The First House describes how you enter the world and how others first experience you. It reflects your
     instinctive approach to new situations, your visible manner, and the developing sense of self through
     which the rest of the chart is expressed.


 You may come across as steady, grounded, and reassuring, preferring to find your own rhythm
 before moving forward. Familiar comforts support your confidence, while small adventures remind
 you that security can travel with you.




 Second House - Values and Resources
 House of Values


     HOUSE II                                                ♊ GEMINI 14.0°
     Money, possessions, personal resources, values,
     self-worth, and material security.                    Mutable · Air

     The Second House explores what you value and what helps you feel secure. It describes your relationship
     with money and possessions, the skills and resources you can rely on, and the inner sense of worth that
     shapes how you build stability.


 You may explore several ways to earn or use your skills, valuing knowledge and flexibility as much
 as material possessions. A simple plan can help varied interests support your security without
 scattering your resources.

 Third House - Communication and Learning
 House of Communication


     HOUSE III                                               ♋ CANCER 5.4°
     Communication, everyday learning, siblings,
     neighbours, short journeys, and your immediate         Cardinal · Water
     environment.

     The Third House describes how you gather, organise, and exchange everyday information. It relates to
     your communication style, early learning, siblings and peers, local surroundings, and the curiosity that
     connects one experience to the next.


 You may communicate with feeling and remember the emotional meaning of everyday encounters.
 A familiar learning environment can help you open up, while checking assumptions keeps sensitivity
 from turning another person's tone into a whole story.

 ♃ Jupiter 7.1° · • Chiron 12.2°




 Fourth House - Home and Roots
 House of Home


     HOUSE IV                                                ♋ CANCER 26.5°
     Home, family, ancestry, privacy, belonging, and
     emotional foundations.                                 Cardinal · Water

     The Fourth House represents your private foundation. It speaks to home, family and ancestry, the
     conditions that help you feel safe, and the inner base you return to when the outer world becomes
     demanding.


 You may place deep value on belonging, family bonds, and the emotional atmosphere of your home.
 Caring for your private world can be restorative, while boundaries help you honour your roots without
 carrying every family feeling.

 ☽ Moon 15.3° · • Mean South Node 12.0° · • True South Node 12.0°

 Fifth House - Creativity and Joy
 House of Creativity


     HOUSE V                                                 ♌ LEO 21.9°
     Creativity, pleasure, play, romance, children, and
     heartfelt self-expression.                             Fixed · Fire

     The Fifth House is the realm of creative vitality and personal joy. It describes how you play, take
     expressive risks, enjoy romance, relate to children, and make something uniquely your own simply
     because it feels alive.


 You may feel most alive when creating, performing, playing, or sharing affection with an open heart.
 Recognition can feel lovely, but the pleasure of expression deserves a place even when nobody is
 watching.




 Sixth House - Daily Life and Wellbeing
 House of Daily Practice


     HOUSE VI                                                ♍ VIRGO 27.9°
     Daily routines, work, service, wellbeing, practical
     skills, and responsibilities.                          Mutable · Earth

     The Sixth House concerns the practices that sustain everyday life. It describes work habits, service,
     practical responsibilities, skill development, and the routines through which you care for your body and
     make your efforts useful.


 You may find comfort in practical routines and making everyday life work a little better. Small,
 sustainable habits can support you, especially when you leave room for imperfection and recognise
 that rest is useful too.

 Seventh House - Partnership
 House of Partnership


     HOUSE VII                                               ♏ SCORPIO 16.2°
     Committed relationships, cooperation, agreements,
     equality, and one-to-one encounters.                   Fixed · Water

     The Seventh House describes how you meet others as equals. It relates to close partnership, commitment,
     cooperation and agreements, while revealing the qualities you seek, admire, negotiate with, or discover
     through another person.


 You may seek depth, loyalty, and emotional honesty in close partnerships. Trust matters deeply,
 and relationships can invite you to balance intimacy with healthy boundaries and space for each
 person's independence.

 ♇ Pluto 16.5° · • Mean Lilith 20.0°




 Eighth House - Intimacy and Transformation
 House of Transformation


     HOUSE VIII                                              ♐ SAGITTARIUS 14.0°
     Intimacy, trust, shared resources, psychological
     depth, endings, and renewal.                           Mutable · Fire

     The Eighth House explores what is shared, hidden, or changed through deep involvement. It concerns
     intimacy and trust, shared finances and obligations, psychological insight, and the process of releasing
     an old form so that renewal can begin.


 You may approach vulnerability, shared resources, and life's transitions through a search for
 meaning. Exploring different perspectives can help you face uncertainty, while practical agreements
 keep shared commitments grounded.

 Ninth House - Meaning and Exploration
 House of Meaning


     HOUSE IX                                                ♑ CAPRICORN 5.4°
     Beliefs, higher learning, philosophy, travel,
     teaching, and the search for meaning.                 Cardinal · Earth

     The Ninth House expands the horizon of the mind. It describes higher education, travel and cultural
     exchange, philosophy and belief, and the experiences through which you form a larger understanding of
     life and your place within it.


 You may approach education, beliefs, and long-term exploration with seriousness and a desire for a
 solid foundation. Disciplined study can be rewarding, while encounters outside established
 frameworks can add richness to what you already know.

 ♄ Saturn 25.3° · ♅ Uranus 9.5° · ♆ Neptune 14.5°




 Tenth House - Vocation and Public Life
 House of Vocation


     HOUSE X                                                 ♑ CAPRICORN 26.5°
     Career, vocation, reputation, responsibility,
     achievement, and public contribution.                 Cardinal · Earth

     The Tenth House represents your visible contribution and the direction of mature ambition. It speaks to
     vocation, reputation, responsibility and achievement, and to the role you gradually build in the wider
     world.


 You may approach ambition with patience, discipline, and a desire to earn lasting respect. Building
 steadily can be meaningful, while defining success for yourself leaves room for a fulfilling life
 alongside achievement.

 • Mean Node 12.0° · • True Node 12.0°

 Eleventh House - Community and Vision
 House of Community


     HOUSE XI                                                 ♒ AQUARIUS 21.9°
     Friendship, networks, community, collective causes,
     future hopes, and shared visions.                      Fixed · Air

     The Eleventh House connects personal life with the wider group. It describes friendship, networks and
     communities, participation in shared causes, and the future-oriented hopes that grow when people
     imagine and create together.


 You may be drawn to communities that welcome individuality, new ideas, and social change. Your
 vision can help a group imagine a different future, while attentive personal connection keeps
 collective ideals rooted in real relationships.

 ♀ Venus 27.8° · ♂ Mars 8.4°




 Twelfth House - Inner Life and Release
 House of the Inner World


     HOUSE XII                                                ♓ PISCES 27.9°
     Solitude, spirituality, dreams, compassion, healing,
     retreat, and unconscious patterns.                     Mutable · Water

     The Twelfth House describes the quieter territory beneath ordinary awareness. It relates to solitude,
     dreams, spirituality and compassion, as well as patterns that need reflection, healing, or release before a
     new cycle begins.


 You may have a vivid inner life and feel restored by imagination, contemplation, or compassionate
 connection. Grounding habits and clear boundaries help you move between your private world and
 everyday life without feeling overwhelmed.

 ☉ Sun 11.5° · ☿ Mercury 14.4°
