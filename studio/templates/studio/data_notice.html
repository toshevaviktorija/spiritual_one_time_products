Your data stays private. We save your name, email address, birth details, billing details and request, delivery and invoice records to prepare and deliver your chart. JSON and SVG inputs are processed temporarily. Your PDF is held privately while we prepare and check it, then removed from this website after the email is submitted for delivery. We do not keep a permanent archive of your natal chart.

You can request removal of your saved data using the button in your chart email. Requests are reviewed by the Venastella team before deletion. Sending uses our email provider, Resend; already-delivered emails and downloaded copies are separate from data held on this website.

If you request deletion, we remove your chart and profile data. Legally required invoice and transaction records remain in our restricted accounting records for the applicable retention period. These records contain billing information, not your natal chart or birth details.
